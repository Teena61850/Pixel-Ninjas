NEON ORB FRENZY

Pop glowing orbs, unleash power-ups, protect your lives and climb the leaderboard.

HOW TO PLAY

You have 5 lives and 60 seconds. Pop positive orbs, avoid red danger orbs, and collect power-ups before they disappear.
